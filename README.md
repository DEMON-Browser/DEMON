### About DEMON

DEMON is a privacy-focused browser built to protect your information from skids, trackers, and unwanted surveillance.  
Every session is designed to keep you hidden and in control.

---

### Features

- **Fang VPN** – Built-in private WireGuard VPN
- **Ultra Private Mode** – New identity generated every time you close the browser
- **Ad & Tracker Blocker** – Blocks ads and tracking by default
- **Privacy Tab** – Isolated private browsing
- **No logs • No tracking • No compromise**

---

### Why choose DEMON?

We don't use emails as accounts, we use random IDs that gets deleted after you end a session with DEMON. The reason we do this is because so you can't track activities including search history.
